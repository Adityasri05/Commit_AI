# Responsible AI and Ethics

Responsible AI is the practice of designing, developing, deploying, and maintaining AI systems in ways that are safe, fair, transparent, privacy-conscious, and accountable.

## Core principles

### Fairness
Evaluate whether system outcomes differ unfairly across relevant groups. Use appropriate data, metrics, and human review.

### Privacy and data protection
Collect only necessary data, protect it throughout its lifecycle, and respect consent and applicable data-protection requirements.

### Transparency and explainability
Communicate what a system can and cannot do. Provide explanations or provenance where they help people understand consequential outputs.

### Safety and robustness
Test systems against errors, adversarial inputs, misuse, and unexpected operating conditions. Monitor after deployment.

### Accountability
Assign clear ownership for decisions, incident response, audits, and ongoing maintenance.

### Human oversight
Keep qualified people involved in high-impact decisions. Provide ways to challenge or escalate consequential outcomes.

## Practical review checklist
- Is the intended use clearly defined?
- Are data sources lawful, relevant, and documented?
- Have limitations and likely failure modes been tested?
- Could the system expose private or sensitive information?
- Are outputs evaluated across relevant user groups?
- Can users report problems and obtain human review?
- Is there a monitoring and incident-response plan?

Responsible AI is not a one-time checklist. It requires continuous evaluation as data, users, models, and deployment conditions change.
